# Public References

## Purpose and use

These public sources inform the project's terminology, risk questions, security considerations, lifecycle framing, and future direction. The AI Governance Control Plane uses independently written logic and synthetic examples. A citation indicates influence or context, not endorsement, conformance, certification, or wholesale adoption.

## OWASP

- OWASP GenAI Security Project. [*State of Agentic AI Security and Governance*, Version 2.01](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/state-of-agentic-ai-security-and-governance/), June 2026. Used as context for agent taxonomy, identity and privilege, tool invocation, memory and state, cross-boundary operation, runtime monitoring, incident routing, and containment.
- OWASP GenAI Security Project. [*OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications for 2026*](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-agentic-applications-for-2026/), December 2025. Used to identify agent-specific risk questions involving goals, tools, identity, supply chains, code execution, memory, inter-agent communication, cascading failures, human-agent trust, and rogue behavior.
- OWASP GenAI Security Project. [*OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025*](https://genai.owasp.org/resource/owasp-top-10-for-llm-applications-2025/), November 2024. Used as security context for LLM and generative-AI applications across development, deployment, and management.

## NIST

- National Institute of Standards and Technology. [*Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1), January 2023. Used for lifecycle-oriented, context-sensitive management of risks to individuals, organizations, and society.
- National Institute of Standards and Technology. [*Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile*, NIST AI 600-1](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1), July 2024. Used for generative-AI risk considerations and actions aligned to the AI RMF functions.

## ISO and IEC

- ISO/IEC. [*ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Information technology, Artificial intelligence, Management system*](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001). Used as management-system context for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving organizational AI governance.
- ISO/IEC. [*ISO/IEC 23894:2023, Information technology, Artificial intelligence, Guidance on risk management*](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html). Used as context for integrating AI risk management into organizational activities and functions.

ISO standards text is copyrighted and is not reproduced in this repository. The links above lead to official overview or publication pages, and access to full standards may require purchase or authorized organizational access.

## Citation maintenance

Review links, editions, and superseding publications before a public release or material methodology revision. Record substantive source-driven changes in repository history and preserve the distinction between public-source influence and independently authored project logic.
